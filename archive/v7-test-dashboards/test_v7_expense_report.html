Observations on economics

Node operator earnings (from the stress suite): roughly 0.84 SOL flowed into 5 NodeVault PDAs across ~600 rounds. That's an average of 0.168 SOL per device, which an operator could withdraw to their configured payout wallet via the withdraw_from_vault instruction. On mainnet, a device that served 1 000 000 rounds a year would earn 0.00028 × 1 000 000 = 280 SOL/year gross (at 1.4 M lamports ÷ 5 nodes per round — scales down as more nodes join the cluster).
The 0.002 SOL fee is a constant in the code (REQUEST_FEE_LAMPORTS in programs/dice/src/constants.rs). Pricing adjustments require a program upgrade. Recommend adding a governance-controlled protocol_config account in v8 to let this be tuned without a redeploy.
The stress test was cheap. Running the entire category A + B + E + F + G + I suite across ~600 real devnet rounds cost about 0.10 SOL of genuinely non-recoverable network fees. Most of the pool "drain" is either still in our wallets, in the node vaults (which ARE our devices), or locked in PDAs that can be closed.
