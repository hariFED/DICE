Payment Model & Economics

How DICE VRF pricing, fees, and node economics work

The Price: 0.002 SOL Per Request

0.002 SOL

~$0.30 at $150/SOL · 2,000,000 lamports · Fixed protocol fee

Every randomness request costs exactly 0.002 SOL. This fee is paid by the developer (or their user) when calling request_randomness. The fee is locked in an on-chain escrow PDA and distributed after the round completes.

For Judges: Why This Pricing Works

Competitive positioning

0.002 SOL matches Switchboard's VRF pricing exactly, but with a fundamentally different security model (hardware TRNG vs Intel SGX TEE). Chainlink VRF is not on Solana. ORAO is cheaper (0.001 SOL) but software-only with no hardware entropy guarantee.

Sustainable economics

The 70/20/10 split ensures node operators are incentivized (70%), the protocol can fund development (20%), and there's a safety reserve (10%). At scale with 100 nodes handling 10,000 requests/day, the protocol earns 4 SOL/day in treasury revenue.

DePIN alignment

Node hardware costs $8 — dramatically cheaper than any DePIN competitor (Helium $200-500, DIMO $99, Hivemapper $300). ROI is under 1 hour at sustained utilization. This makes the node network highly scalable.
