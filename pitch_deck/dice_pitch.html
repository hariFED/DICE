DICE

A randomness oracle rooted in silicon.

01 · Physical entropy

Five ESP32-S3 devices, each with a hardware RNG and a secp256k1 device key burned into NVS. Entropy is generated on the chip, not in a process.

02 · Commit-reveal across mTLS

Each device sends a SHA-256 commit, then reveals the entropy + ECDSA signature only after all commits are bound. Front-running is impossible by construction.

03 · On-chain finality

An Anchor program verifies each signature, hashes the entropies, and writes the result + per-node payouts atomically. Devnet program ID 78Qv6cyKkRZN2YngiLSSBCe2iyRc6jgtCs3incCaMRcv.

How it works

A request, end to end, in one slide.

  • 1 · dApp calls request_randomness_auto on a pre-funded DiceChannel PDA.
  • 2 · Coordinator selects 4 of 5 active devices via on-chain select_nodes + SlotHashes randomness.
  • 3 · Each device generates entropy on its RNG, signs SHA-256(entropy), sends the commit over mTLS.
  • 4 · After all commits land on-chain, devices reveal the entropy + ECDSA signature.
  • 5 · Program verifies each signature, hashes the entropies, writes the result, splits the fee 70/20/10 in one atomic TX.
Why each layer matters
  • Hardware: entropy never leaves the chip in plaintext until the reveal phase.
  • mTLS: the wire is encrypted; the device cert binds identity to silicon.
  • Commit-reveal: the coordinator can't see entropy before the commits are sealed on-chain.
  • On-chain finality: every reveal is verified by the Solana program; the result is just SHA-256(e₁‖e₂‖e₃‖e₄).
Bias-free as long as at least one device honestly contributes entropy — not "as long as ⅔ of nodes are honest."

Defensible moat

The crypto isn't the moat. The fleet is.

Operational PKI

Private step-ca, secp256r1 mTLS certs + secp256k1 device identity, split-key NVS provisioning. Years of cert hygiene to copy.

Hardware supply chain

A bonded device manufacturer + provisioning pipeline. New entrants can't ship a 5-node network in a weekend.

Verifiable boot

Bounded firmware monitor, signed binaries, factory-reset via PKI revocation. Every device is a known-good silicon block.

Audit-grade story

A regulator can trace any random bit back to a specific board, a specific NVS, a specific cert. No software DON can say that.

Cypherpunk credibility

Closest analogs in the corpus are TEENet, ASSAP, WeTEE, SUMOTEX. Hardware-rooted trust is a recognised movement.

Streaming primitive

RandomnessFeed PDA + crank — passive subscribers (e.g. pulse) read randomness with zero round-trip. No competitor has this shape.
