Guidelines.

This document is the long version of: please don't add gradients. DICE is an editorial, mono-palette, pixel-mixed brand — born from ASCII terminals and technical drawings. Keep that shape and it reads right.

chapter · 02 · logo02 / 06

The mark.

An isometric cube with a top-right sub-cube offset. Reads as a dice, a block, a stack, a unit — a single token of entropy.

Primary lockups

dark · primary · on 0A0A0A
light · inverse · on FFFFFF

Clear space

Minimum clear space on all sides is equal to the height of the sub-cube in the mark. No text, imagery, or other marks inside that zone. Minimum render size is 20 px square for digital, 8 mm for print.

Do / Don't

DO

Render in pure mono

White on dark. Black on light. Use currentColor so it inherits the surrounding text color.
DON'T

Don't color the cube

No gradients, no chrome, no brand-accent tint. The mark is geometric — color diminishes it.
DO

Pair with DICE wordmark

On primary surfaces. Use Silkscreen (pixel) or set the wordmark in uppercase Geist Sans tracked tight.
DON'T

Don't rotate or skew

The isometric angle is canonical. Rotation flattens its geometry; skews break the sub-cube readout.

chapter · 05 · voice05 / 06

How DICE speaks.

Short sentences. Hardware specifics. No marketing fog.

Principles

DO

Name the hardware

"ESP32-S3" · "secp256k1" · "commit-reveal". The specificity is the substance.
DON'T

Adjective stacking

"Revolutionary, next-gen, enterprise-grade" — cut them. The chip doesn't care, neither do builders.
DO

Prefer numbers

"3.9 s avg · 50-round bench" beats "extremely fast". Quote measured, publish the methodology.
DON'T

Pretend to be bigger

20 nodes is 20 nodes. Pre-orders are pre-orders. Describe what is, roadmap what isn't — never blur them.

Sample lines

✓   Hardware-backed randomness for Solana. 0.002 SOL per request.

✓   20 physical ESP32-S3 devices. Commit-reveal across the mesh. Sub-four-second VRF rounds.

✕   The world's most advanced decentralized randomness ecosystem. (too puffed)
