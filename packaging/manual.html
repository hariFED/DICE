02 — Setup

Get Your Node Online in 3 Minutes

1

Power On

Plug the USB-C cable into the DICE Node and connect to any USB power source (5V, 500mA minimum). The LED will light up blue — the node is ready to configure.

2

Connect to WiFi Setup

On your phone or laptop, open WiFi settings and connect to:

DICE-XXXX

The last 4 characters match your device. A setup page will open automatically. If it doesn't, navigate to:

http://192.168.4.1
3

Enter WiFi & Your Solana Wallet

Select your home/office WiFi network, enter the password, and paste your Solana wallet address — this is where your node earnings (0.002 SOL per VRF request) will be sent. The node saves everything securely in encrypted storage and restarts.

⚠ Requires a 2.4 GHz WiFi network. 5 GHz-only networks are not supported by the ESP32-S3.
4

Node Goes Online

After restarting, the LED turns yellow while connecting, then solid green when online and registered with the DICE Network coordinator. Your node is now earning by serving verifiable randomness.

05 — How It Works

Verifiable Random Function (VRF)

Your DICE Node generates provably fair, tamper-proof random numbers for Solana smart contracts. Here's what happens when a dApp requests randomness:

①

Request

A Solana dApp calls the DICE smart contract requesting randomness. The coordinator assigns nodes.

②

Commit

Your node generates entropy from 3 hardware sources, creates a cryptographic commitment, and sends the hash on-chain.

③

Reveal

After all nodes commit, each reveals its entropy. The values are combined into a single verifiable random output.

06 — Troubleshooting

Common Issues

LED stays blue — can't find DICE-XXXX WiFi network

Make sure you are within 3 meters of the node. Try turning your phone's WiFi off and on. The AP name is case-sensitive and starts with "DICE-".

LED turns yellow but never goes green

The node can't connect to your WiFi or the coordinator. Verify your WiFi password is correct and that you're on a 2.4 GHz network. Check that your router allows outbound WebSocket connections.

LED is red

A fatal error occurred. Unplug the node, wait 5 seconds, and reconnect. If the red LED persists, hold the BOOT button for 10 seconds to factory reset, then re-run WiFi setup.

How do I change WiFi networks?

Hold the BOOT button for 10 seconds. The LED will turn blue, indicating the setup portal is active again. Connect to DICE-XXXX and enter new credentials.

How do I check my node's earnings?

During setup, you entered your own Solana wallet address. Search that address on any Solana explorer (like solscan.io or explorer.solana.com) to see your VRF fee earnings and transaction history.

07 — Safety & Care

Important Notes

ℹ GPIO 1 must remain unconnected. This pin samples analog noise for entropy generation. Connecting anything to it will degrade randomness quality.
  • Keep the node in a ventilated area — avoid enclosing it in airtight spaces
  • Use only USB-C cables for power — do not apply external voltage to GPIO pins
  • The node is designed for indoor use only
  • Do not open or modify the enclosure — doing so voids any warranty
  • Keep firmware up to date — OTA updates will be announced via the DICE Network dashboard
